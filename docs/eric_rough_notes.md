# Project 2

Name: Seedkeepers

Community Library for Seeds

Batches of Seeds known as a *seed lot*

each *seed lot* has a lineage:

- what it is
-

Store seed lineage/ancestry

Seed testing occurs

Busiest time of the year is february

Catalog is public
member information is private

Some donors want their names associated with their donated seeds,
some don't.

Can either accept or reject seeds that are provided

Usually people who bring in the seeds bring them in an envelope with a handwritten note covering the information that is provided.

what is currently recorded:

- lineage
- variety
- who grew it
- story
- germination status
- packaged
- season
- seed bag (quantity)

Transactions are not automatic they are approval based

Roles:
Administration
Germination
Transaction Authorization

Donors can become a member but they don't have to be.

People who donate have a special view for their donated seed lots legacy.

Users have an address
User request justification
User fertilizer usage
User water quality for seed lot usage
